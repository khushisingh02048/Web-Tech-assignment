Purpose of the <a> Tag in HTML

The <a> tag (anchor tag) is used to create hyperlinks in HTML. It allows users to click and move from one webpage to another.

Syntax:

    <a href="URL" target="value">Link Text</a>

Example:

Visit Google

In this example:

  • href → specifies the link destination (Google website).
  • target="_blank" → opens the link in a new tab.
  • Visit Google → the clickable text shown to the user.

Example of Internal Link:

Go to Bottom of Page

You have reached the bottom of the page!
